Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new task?

Tap the + button on the Today screen. Enter your goal, choose how often it repeats, and set how you want to track it (yes/no or progress-based).

How do I track progress on a task?

Simply tap on a task to add progress. Each tap adds the increment you set when creating the task. For example, if you're tracking water intake with 8oz increments, each tap adds 8oz.

How do I edit or delete a task?

Swipe left on a task to reveal the Edit button. Swipe right to see Delete and Reset options.

How do I reset my progress for today?

When a task is completed (green), tap the reset button that appears on the right side of the task. You can also swipe right and tap Reset.

How do I add the widget to my home screen?

Long-press on your iPhone home screen, tap the + button in the top corner, search for "Busy Bee", and select the Large widget. You can tap tasks directly on the widget!

Why isn't my widget showing my tasks?

Make sure you have tasks created in the app first. The widget shows today's scheduled tasks. Try removing and re-adding the widget if it's not updating.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your Busy Bee data will be included in your device backup.

How do streaks work?

Complete a task every day (or every scheduled occurrence for weekly/monthly tasks) to build a streak. Miss a day, and your streak resets to zero. Your best streak is always saved!
