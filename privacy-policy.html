Privacy Policy

Busy Bee - Daily Habits

Last Updated: January 2026

Overview

Busy Bee is a habit tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Busy Bee is designed with privacy in mind. The App:

  • Does not require an account or registration
  • Does not collect personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services
  • Does not contain advertisements
  • Does not send any data to external servers

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Tasks: The habits and goals you create (title, frequency, target values, icons)
  • Progress Records: Your daily progress and completion history
  • Streaks: Your streak counts for each habit

This data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and remains entirely on your iPhone. If you use the Busy Bee widget, data is shared between the App and the widget through Apple's secure App Group container — this sharing happens entirely on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties.

Since we don't collect any data, there is nothing to share. Your habit data never leaves your device.

Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • On-device storage only: No cloud servers, no remote databases
  • iOS Security: Your data is protected by Apple's iOS security features, including device encryption
  • No network transmission: The App does not transmit data over the internet

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual tasks: Swipe to delete any habit
  • Reset progress: Reset progress on any task at any time
  • Delete the App: Uninstalling Busy Bee removes all associated data from your device

Children's Privacy

Busy Bee does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The App is safe for users of all ages.

iCloud and Backup

If you back up your iPhone using iCloud or iTunes/Finder, your Busy Bee data may be included in that backup. This is controlled by your device settings and Apple's backup features, not by our App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
